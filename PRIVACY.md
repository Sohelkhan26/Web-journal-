# Chronicle — Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty Charter

**Effective Date:** October 2, 2026

At Chronicle, we believe personal journal entries, private thoughts, sketches, and daily reflections are sacred and belong exclusively to you. This Privacy Policy explains our strict privacy-by-design architecture, zero-tracking commitment, and how you retain total ownership of your data.

---

## 🕊️ Core Privacy Principles

1. **Local-First by Default**: All writing, drawings, stickers, and attached photos reside directly on your device in your browser's private IndexedDB storage.
2. **Zero Telemetry**: We do not collect analytics, telemetry, user profiles, or behavior metrics.
3. **Zero Third-Party Trackers**: No Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, advertising SDKs, or session recorders.
4. **No Cookies**: Chronicle does not set tracking or marketing cookies.
5. **Absolute Data Sovereignty**: You can export your data in standard JSON format or erase it permanently at any time.

---

## 📦 What Information is Processed?

### 1. Information Processed Locally on Your Device
- **Journal Entries**: Text blocks, page titles, dates, fonts, ink colors, and line configurations.
- **Sketches & Art**: Vector stroke coordinates and pressure data generated by mouse, stylus, or drawing tablets.
- **Attached Photos**: Images you choose to upload or pin as Polaroids are compressed client-side before being saved into your local IndexedDB storage.
- **Preferences**: Leather cover style, paper styles, lighting mode, ambient sound settings, and bookmarks.

None of this information is transmitted to our servers during regular local usage.

### 2. Information Processed During Cloud Synchronization (Optional)
If and only if you explicitly choose to configure Cloudflare Edge Sync (`/api/sync`):
- Your journal pages and compressed media are transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 to Cloudflare D1.
- If you supply a **Sync Passkey**, Chronicle uses a cryptographic hash (`SHA-256`) to isolate your database partition.
- Neither Cloudflare nor the creators of Chronicle access, index, or sell your entries.

---

## 🌐 External Sub-Processors & CDNs

To render high-quality typography and tactile assets, the app connects to the following essential infrastructure:
- **Cloudflare Pages & Edge CDN**: Delivers the compiled web application assets with DDoS protection and edge caching. (Subject to Cloudflare's Privacy Policy).
- **Google Fonts CDN**: Downloads the authentic handwriting typography (*Caveat, Kalam, Shadows Into Light, Courier Prime, Playfair Display*). No cookies are sent with font requests.
- **Unsplash (Optional)**: If you select a curated keepsake preset photo, the image is fetched securely from Unsplash over HTTPS.

---

## 🇪🇺 GDPR & 🇺🇸 CCPA Rights

Chronicle is built to naturally exceed the privacy requirements of the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

- **Right of Access & Portability**: Click the **Privacy** button in the header or the **Storage / Sync** button to immediately export your complete journal as a standard JSON file.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: In the **Privacy Center** > **Data Storage & Erasure** tab, click **Erase All Data on Device**. This permanently destroys all pages, photos, and settings from your browser's storage.
- **Right to Rectification**: All entries can be edited, re-indexed, or torn out directly within the journal interface.
- **Right to Object / Restrict Processing**: Because we collect no personal data or analytics, no processing occurs that requires restriction.

---

## 🔒 Security of Your Information

- Strict **Content Security Policy (CSP)** to prevent unauthorized external scripts from accessing your IndexedDB storage.
- **X-Frame-Options: DENY** to eliminate clickjacking risk.
- **HTTPS & HSTS** enforcement on all production network transmissions.
- Client-side image compression to prevent memory exhaustion and excessive storage consumption.

---

## 🧒 Children's Privacy

Chronicle is safe for writers of all ages. Because we do not collect personal identifying information (PII), we do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13.
